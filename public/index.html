❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to shipping, warranty, returns, and payments.

📦 What is your dispatch and delivery timeframe?

Orders are packaged and dispatched from our logistics hub within 24 to 48 hours. Standard courier shipping arrives in 2–4 business days with end-to-end tracking.

🛡️ How does the 2-Year Hardware Warranty work?

Every purchase includes a no-hassle 2-year warranty covering acoustic driver failures, battery degradation below 80%, PCB errors, and manufacturing defects. We issue an instant pre-paid replacement.

🔄 What is the 30-Day Zero Questions Return Policy?

Try any device in your setup for up to 30 days. If it doesn’t suit your workflow, initiate a return from the Orders page or email support. You will receive a 100% refund with zero restocking penalties.

💳 What payment options are supported?

We accept Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal Express, and Cash on Delivery (COD) upon courier arrival.
